03 / SOLVE A w = k, THEN KEEP EVERY TERM

Not every weight is positive.

The conditional mean is m + sum_i w_i(y_i - m). A target change delta_i contributes w_i delta_i. These are sensitivities to observed targets under fixed coordinates, kernel, mean, noise and jitter. They are not normalized attention, probabilities, feature attribution or causal influence. Negative weights and sums different from one are allowed.

04 / THE ASSUMPTIONS ARE PART OF THE ANSWER

The native path uses the original Cholesky conditioning and direct edited-target prediction. The browser independently factors the same covariance with positive-pivot unit-lower LDL^T, then solves for every response weight. Numerical residuals are checked; failed pivots, invalid inputs or material negative variance refuse without adaptive jitter or matrix repair. Small permitted negative diagonals may clamp to zero under the original documented tolerance.
Changing kernel settings, observed locations, noise, the supplied mean or learning hyperparameters from edited targets would change this experiment. A small residual is not a forward-error bound. This witness carries the complete public synthetic model, not a source-authenticity signature.
