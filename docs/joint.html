The numerical computation stays explicit

Native conditioning uses the original Cholesky solver. This browser independently uses unit-lower LDLᵀ solves. It groups identical latent coordinates before the projected prior-minus-conditioning subtraction, then compares with the direct full-covariance contraction. Small negative variances may clamp under a reported gate; raw signed values and disagreements stay in the JSON. No adaptive jitter, covariance repair or PSD certificate.

Training jitter changes the conditioned model but is not future observation noise. Ill-conditioned inputs may fail. A small numerical residual is not a relative or forward-error bound, especially for a contrast near zero. No probability of all queries being inside their pointwise intervals is computed.
